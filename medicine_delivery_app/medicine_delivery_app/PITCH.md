# Doorstep Meds

## The problem

Patients who collect regular medication may need to travel to a clinic and wait in a queue. This can be difficult for people who have limited mobility or live far from the clinic.

## The proposed approach

Doorstep Meds is a prototype for managing deliveries from a clinic to patients in their village. Clinic staff prepare a parcel and assign it to a messenger. The messenger collects the parcel and takes it to the recipient. A PIN is used to confirm that the delivery has been completed.

## Main features

- Clinic and messenger accounts
- Messenger registration and approval by clinic staff
- Delivery creation and status tracking
- PIN confirmation at delivery
- Messages between clinic staff and messengers

## Current project status

The application was developed as an FNB App Academy capstone. The current version is intended for demonstration and assessment, not for use with real patients. The SMS and email integrations have not been verified with live service accounts.

## Possible future work

Further work could include testing with clinic staff and messengers, a properly configured SMS service, offline support, improved reporting, a production database and an independent privacy and security review.
