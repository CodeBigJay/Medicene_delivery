# Doorstep Meds: pitch (fill the [brackets] with your own facts)

**The problem.** Elderly and disabled patients on chronic medication must travel to the clinic every month and wait in
long queues. For many that means paying for transport, missing a day of care, or skipping collection.

**The solution.** The clinic packs the parcel. A trusted messenger from the patient's own village collects it and
delivers it to the door. The patient (or caregiver) confirms with a PIN sent by SMS. No smartphone is needed by the patient.

**Why it can work here.** Messengers already know the village. The PIN proves the right person received it.
Messengers never see what the medicine is.

**Evidence to collect (do not skip, this is what judges remember)**
- Queue time at [your local clinic]: [x minutes], source: [who told you / what you observed]
- Transport cost for a patient from [village] to the clinic: [R x return]
- One quote from a nurse, a patient or a caregiver: "[...]"
- Number of people who tested the app and what they said (see PEER_FEEDBACK.md)

**Pilot plan.** 1 clinic, 2 villages, 2 messengers, 10 patients, 4 weeks. Measure: parcels delivered, failed deliveries,
clinic queue time before and after, patient and messenger satisfaction.

**Next steps.** Real SMS gateway, offline mode, delivery reports for the clinic, more clinics (PostgreSQL), POPIA review.
